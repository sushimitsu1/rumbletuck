A note from the dad behind Rumbletuck

Every night my little one asked for “one more truck story,” and every night we ran out of books. So I started making them up: a yellow digger who goes ra-ta-ta-ta, a night train that never hurries, a tiny dinghy who helps her big friend find his way home.

Those made-up stories became our favorite part of the day. It wasn’t really about the trucks. It was the snuggles, the giggles at the sound words, and the sleepy “again!”

Rumbletuck is how I’m sharing them with your family. Every story is gentle, about two minutes long, and ends with everyone settling down to sleep. I hope they give you a few more cozy minutes together.

Goodnight, and sweet dreams. 🌙
— the dad behind Rumbletuck

P.S. No ads, no accounts, no trackers. Just stories.

Things to do together

Little ideas for the minutes before (and after) the story.

🔍 Guess the vehicle

Read the clue out loud. Can your little one guess who it is?

📣 Say the sound words

Every story has a sound to make together. Let your little one be the vehicle:

  • Ra-ta-ta-ta! Ra’s breaker
  • Whooooosh! the windy park in Gavin’s story
  • Swish, swish, swish! Cody’s turning mixer
  • Scrrrape–swish! Niko’s snowplow
