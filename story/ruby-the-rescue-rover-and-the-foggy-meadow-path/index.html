The story

One soft evening, Ruby the Rescue Rover rolled into Meadow Hollow. Her amber roof lights glowed warm.

She began her gentle check of the quiet hills. Birds settled in the tall grass.

A gentle fog drifted over the path. Ruby slowed and listened carefully.

Then Ruby heard a tiny “baa.” A little lamb was lost beside the clover.

“I’m Lulu,” the lamb whispered. “I can’t see Mama’s shed in this fog.”

Ruby smiled kindly. “I can help with my warm lights,” she said.

Lulu walked close beside Ruby. Together they followed the soft dirt path.

Ruby’s amber lights made a warm golden path through the mist. Lulu felt braver.

They passed a crooked fence post. Beyond it, a yellow porch lantern blinked.

“I found the path home!” Lulu said. Ruby rolled steady and safe.

Soon the wooden meadow shed appeared. Mama Sheep waited on the porch.

Mama nuzzled Lulu gently. “Thank you for bringing my lamb home safe.”

Lulu’s blue ribbon shone in the lantern light. The meadow felt peaceful again.

Ruby parked by the clover. The porch lantern glowed like a tiny moon.

That night, Ruby rested under the quiet moon. Goodnight, Ruby. Goodnight, Lulu.

The End. Sweet dreams! 🌙
