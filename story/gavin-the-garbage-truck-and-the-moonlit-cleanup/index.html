Gavin the Garbage Truck and the Moonlit Cleanup

After a windy gust, Gavin tidies the park so the little animals can sleep. A gentle, 15-page picture book with a calm narrator, about 2 minutes long and made for winding down at bedtime.

The story

The moon was rising over the village park. Gavin the Garbage Truck rolled by for his calm evening check.

The picnic tables were quiet and still. The little park animals were getting ready for bed.

Then whooooosh! A windy gust swirled through the park. Cups and paper fluttered everywhere.

Wrappers slid by the benches. A bottle rolled near the path. The park was messy now!

Gavin blinked his big brown eyes. ‘Oh dear,’ he said. ‘I will clean it all up.’

First Gavin picked up cups and paper near the picnic tables. He worked slowly and carefully.

Then Gavin gathered cans and bottles by the path. A little bunny watched from the grass.

Gavin sorted the recycling into one bin and the trash into another. He liked keeping things neat.

Soon the park looked cleaner and calmer. But Gavin checked once more for any little bits.

Near the flowers, Gavin spotted one last little wrapper. He picked it up gently with his careful grabber.

Then Gavin looked under the table and beside the path. Everything was neat and tidy now.

The little bunny smiled in the grass. The sleepy owl gave a soft happy hoot for Gavin.

Gavin rolled the bins back to their places. The park paths were tidy, safe, and clear.

Soft moonlight shone on the peaceful park. Gavin felt proud and calm inside.

“Goodnight, park,” whispered Gavin. Then he rolled home softly as the little animals slept.

The End. Sweet dreams! 🌙
