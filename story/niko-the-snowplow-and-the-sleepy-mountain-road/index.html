The story

Snowy evening came to Pine Peak Road. Niko the Snowplow blinked his lights and smiled.

Soft snow drifted down. The mountain road grew quiet, twisty, and white.

Niko saw little tracks in the snow. Cars and friends would need a safe path home.

He lowered his plow and pushed the first fluffy drift. Scrrrape–swish! The road peeked through.

Around the bend he cleared a curvy lane. Warm cabin lights twinkled below.

A tiny red car waited by the hill. “Thank you, Niko!” it beeped.

Niko brushed snow from the bridge rails and the signs. He worked slowly and carefully.

At the highest turn, a big sleepy drift lay across the road. Niko took a deep, brave breath.

With one gentle, steady push, he opened the last bend. The road to the mountain lodge glowed ahead.

Niko pushed the last big pile and looked up the road. The lodge was almost there!

Soon the road was clear and smooth. Warm lodge lights twinkled through the snow.

A family waved from the porch. “Thank you, Niko!” they called with happy smiles.

Niko made one more careful pass. He wanted every turn to be safe and neat.

Then Niko parked by the lodge as soft snowflakes floated down. The mountain road was peaceful again.

The stars twinkled above the sleepy mountain road. Goodnight, Niko. Goodnight, snowy night.

The End. Sweet dreams! 🌙
