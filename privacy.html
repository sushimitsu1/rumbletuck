Privacy note

Last updated: October 31, 2026

What we collect

Only the email address a parent or guardian types into the waitlist form, if they choose to. We use it only to send news about the full library launch. You can unsubscribe at any time from any email we send, or ask us to delete your address.

Children

This site is meant to be used by children together with a grown-up. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Reading the stories needs no account or sign-up, and the site has no ads, no tracking pixels and no third-party analytics.

Who handles the waitlist

Waitlist sign-ups are collected and stored by our email provider, Kit (kit.com), and used only to email you about Rumbletuck. After you sign up, Kit sends one email asking you to confirm; if you don't confirm, you won't get anything else from us. We never sell or share email addresses.

Cookies and storage

We do not use cookies, ads, tracking or analytics. Your browser may keep a copy of the site's pages and audio so stories load faster and work offline; that copy stays on your device.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: reply to any waitlist email and we will help.
