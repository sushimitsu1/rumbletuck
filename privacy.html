Privacy note

Last updated: October 31, 2026

What we collect

Only the email address a parent or guardian types into the waitlist form, if they choose to. We use it only to send news about the full library launch. You can unsubscribe at any time from any email we send, or ask us to delete your address.

Children

This site is meant to be used by children together with a grown-up. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Reading the stories needs no account or sign-up, and the site has no ads, no tracking pixels and no third-party analytics. Videos only load from YouTube if you tap play (see Videos below).

Who handles the waitlist

We run the waitlist ourselves. Email addresses are stored in a private database on Cloudflare (cloudflare.com), and our emails are sent through Resend (resend.com). Both only process the address so we can store it and send our emails; neither may use it for anything else. After you sign up, we send one email asking you to confirm. If you don't confirm, you won't get anything else from us, and we delete unconfirmed addresses after 30 days. We never sell or share email addresses.

To stop spam, we briefly keep a scrambled (hashed) version of the network address a sign-up came from, and delete it within two days.

Cookies and storage

We do not use cookies, ads, tracking or analytics. (If you tap play on a video, YouTube may store data in your browser for that video; see Videos.) Your browser may keep a copy of the site's pages and audio so stories load faster and work offline; that copy stays on your device.

Videos

Some pages show our own short videos from YouTube. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you tap play. When you do, the video plays in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), our videos are marked as made for kids, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that video. Links to our YouTube channel open YouTube in a new tab.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: reply to any waitlist email and we will help.
